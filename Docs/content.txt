4. BRAND INTRODUCTION

Small heading:

WELCOME TO THE SIXTH RITU

Some Seasons Change the World.
Others Stay With You.

India has always known six seasons — Vasanta, Grishma, Varsha, Sharad, Hemanta and Shishira.

Each transforms the world around us and leaves behind a feeling.

We imagine another.

A season found in the scent of rain after a storm, the warmth of an evening ritual, flowers resting on silk, or the memory of someone after they have gone.

This is The Sixth Ritu — a season created through fragrance.

CTA: Discover Our Story

The Power After the Storm

Saffron • Rose • Agarwood

A Rose, Made Royal.

03 — GOLDEN RITUAL

Coffee • Vanilla • Amber

Warmth Becomes a Ritual.

04 — GOLDEN LEAF

Tobacco • Honey • Tonka

Wrapped in Golden Warmth.

05 — CRYSTAL BLOOM
